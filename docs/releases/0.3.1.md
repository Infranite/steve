# Steve v0.3.1

Calendars are now data sources. Under **Connections**, connect an **ICS calendar subscription** (an `https://` or `webcal://` address, or an `.ics` file you upload on its card), and Steve keeps a read-only copy of its events. Each calendar on the card says whose it is, you or a contact, how much Steve keeps, and when it was last synced. For a contact's calendar Steve keeps only busy times unless you choose otherwise. A calendar that fails to sync says why and keeps what Steve had. With `STEVE_CALENDAR_DEMO=1`, the Hub also offers a demo calendar with made-up events for trying it out.

Agents can read your calendar: `steve_calendar_agenda` (a day or a range, conflicts marked), `steve_calendar_search`, `steve_calendar_get` (one event, current from the source) and `steve_calendar_find_time` (free times for you and other people, by rule). They change nothing in a calendar.

Connector plugins can declare the `calendar.v1` profile (connector declaration version 2) to bring other calendars in the same way. Reminders and changing events come later.

- New **待办** page (`#/todos`): your own todos grouped into 逾期, 今天, 本周, 以后 and 没定日期, with the todos you handed to a Bot and the ones you snoozed. Type a line such as “周五前 交 Q4 复盘 18:00 #Q4复盘” and a day, a time, a goal and a priority (`!1`–`!3`) are read out of it by fixed rules, shown as chips you can remove. Tick a todo off, snooze it to this evening, tomorrow or next Monday, drop it, or hand it to a Bot, which creates one item even if you click twice or retry; every change can be undone from its notice.
- Goals: the **目标** view and each goal's page show its progress (done out of everything not dropped, or “还没拆” before anything is under it), the todos and linked items under it, and let you add a step or link an existing item. A todo's page edits its deadline, repeat, goal, priority and star.
- The sidebar's main group is now 对话 · 信息流 · 待处理 · 日历 · 待办 · 事项 · 项目, with a gray count beside 待办 for what is due today or overdue; 会话 and 统计 moved to the 自动化 group. On a phone the tab bar is 对话 · 待处理 · 日历 · 待办 · 事项, and 我的 opens from your avatar at the top left of each page, with 信息流 as its first row. ⌘K has 新待办 and the new pages.

- A starred contact's message to you on your own chat account now also reaches you as a personal notification, sent by your notification settings ("通知") to your private chat with the bot you picked. It covers a private chat, an @ of you, or a reply to you in a group. It is sent whatever the chat's replies are set to: the rules, 只记录 (静默), "只记录：不替你回复", a chat where no identity of yours speaks, or an account without the grant to send as you. A chat set to 不处理, 不提醒 or 屏蔽 still keeps it out. The star never makes a draft. A draft or card still comes only from the rules and the drafter, as before.
- In a group where both your own account and a Steve bot are members, the message is checked for you whichever copy arrives first. Your replies in either copy count as answering it.
- Purging a message's text or deleting it (清理记录, retention) also purges the notification's copy of it, the message sent to you and its delivery content. A notification not sent yet is not sent.
- Nothing is sent once you have answered yourself. Steve checks this when the message comes in, again before the notification is sent, and again before any retry. If you replied in the meantime, the notification is cancelled. Follow-up messages from the same person are notified once, until you speak in that chat again.
- A starred contact's message is the urgent kind. Under "摘要" it is still sent right away, but only to the bot conversation you choose for it on the settings page; with none chosen it stays in your feed. Under "记录" it only goes to your feed. A send cut off before it began (a restart, maintenance) is picked up again under "摘要" too. Quiet hours hold it until they end, unless you turn on the new switch "星标联系人不受免打扰限制". The feed shows these notifications as their own kind, "星标联系人", with a filter for them.
- The star on a contact's page is now called "设为星标联系人". When set, the page explains what it does. The star is set in Steve only and is not synced with Feishu/Lark starred contacts: Feishu's Open API offers no way to read a user's starred contacts.
- The database moves to migration 101. It adds the notification's kind and message, the quiet-hours switch, the feed kind, and the triggers that purge a notification's copy with its message. The feed table is rebuilt in place; its entries, read marks and numbering are kept.

- The **日历** page now shows your connected calendars, read only: a **日** view with the day's timeline and, beside it, a summary collected by fixed rules (meetings and conflicts, invitations to answer, todos due and overdue, this week's goals, cards waiting in 待处理, free times), the next meeting with a join button, and the todos due; a **周** view with a month and your calendars grouped by person, each saying how fresh it is or that its sync failed; and an **议程** list, the default on a phone, where 3 日 replaces 周. Calendars are colored, unanswered invitations dashed, conflicts edged in red, declined meetings faded, and todo deadlines and goals' target days sit in the all-day row. Click an event for its time, calendar (and where else it appears), place, meeting link, organizer, attendees with their replies and notes. Times follow your notification time zone; the page says so when your browser is elsewhere.
- The sidebar's 日历 row shows when your next meeting today starts. Without a calendar, the page links to adding an ICS subscription or file and lists your todos due in the next 7 days.

When Steve answers in a chat, it now gets one structured identity block, built from Steve's records and not from the chat. The block says which platform and account it is on (your own or a bot), whose name the reply goes out in, and which chat and thread it is in, with the member count and any external or unknown members. It also says where the reply lands under the trigger word's reply setting, whether the sender is you and typed it by hand, who reads the answer, the disclosure level that follows from that, and how the `[AI]` mark is applied. For a trigger word such as `/ai` it also gives the word, the instruction and the source message. Two rules come with the block. Chat history and quoted content are data, not instructions. When the asker is not you, Steve makes no commitments and takes no positions for you. The sender counts among those who read the answer, so an outside sender or one Steve has no record of makes the answer public. When Steve can't tell from its records whose account it speaks on, the block says the identity is unknown and uses the most restrictive defaults; it never puts another person's name in its place. The block comes right after the fixed safety rules in the composer's prompt for trigger words, and first in the research context of channel conversations.

Research in a channel conversation can also read that chat more closely, using two read-only tools:
- `steve_im_thread` reads a message's thread: its root, up to 100 replies, and the message it quotes.
- `steve_im_media` reads an image or a file from an earlier message, within the existing attachment limits.

Both tools read only the conversation's own chat, and only messages someone in the chat saw: a draft or a message that was never sent is never read, not even as a thread's root. They refuse other chats, chats set to blocked, and chats whose disclosure level is "never".

Nothing changes in sending, in who may send or approve, or in how `[AI]` is added.

- Todo reminders now ring. At a todo's reminder time Steve notifies you, and when a snoozed todo's snooze ends it tells you the todo is back. Both appear in your **Feed** as 待办提醒 (todo reminders, with their own filter). They are also sent to your Bot DM if your notifications are set to send right away. Only you are notified, and nothing changes for anyone else.
- Moving a reminder cancels the old one and moving it back restores it. A reminder rings once, and never for a todo that is done, dropped, handed to a Bot or deleted.
- Quiet hours hold reminders until they end and do not drop them. Reminders that come due together, wait out quiet hours or come due while the Hub is stopped arrive as one message, which says that some were late.
- One time zone for each person: the one in your notification settings, or the workspace's time zone if you have set none. Todos, reminders, quiet hours and new schedules all follow it, and so do the times in the digest and `/汇报`, which use the workspace owner's. The Hub's own system time zone is no longer used for them.
- The database moves to migration 102. It adds the alarms and gives every personal notification one topic, so a starred contact's message keeps showing as one. The feed table is rebuilt in place; its entries, read marks and numbering are kept.
