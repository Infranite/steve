# Steve v0.3.2

- A morning brief. On workdays at 08:30, on your own clock, Steve puts together your day by rule: overdue and due-today todos, starred todos, this week's goals with their progress, how many 待处理 cards wait for you, your running, stuck and recently finished items, and how many notifications you have not read. It appears in your **Feed** as 晨报 (with its own filter), and is also sent to your Bot DM if your notifications are set to send right away.
- It turns on by itself the first time you write a todo, and only you see it. Change its time or turn it off on the Automations page; once you delete it, it does not come back.
- A day with nothing due and no 待处理 is not pushed: the Feed just says 今天没有安排. Quiet hours hold the brief until they end; one that comes after 12:00 stays in the Feed only. After the Hub was stopped, you still get that day's brief, once.

Items gain a derived state, shown in shadow next to today's state. It is computed from the item's facts and puts the item in one of three lanes: needs you, running or done. It also says who moves the item on, what the next step is and when that step comes due. Nothing acts on it yet: who can send, merge or close an item is unchanged, and the item list does not use it.

- The item API (`GET /api/items/{id}`) carries it as `shadow`, together with today's state and whether the two agree. If it cannot be computed, the item API leaves `shadow` out and logs why instead of failing. The item's Debug view shows it under "Derived state (shadow)".
- It keeps apart whether a delivery can be reviewed, merged or closed. A required merge is a condition for closing, not for review. Merging needs your approval of that exact commit. Only facts the Hub checked itself count as hard facts; what the agent reported, or what nobody could tell, does not.
- A late answer from an earlier turn or cycle never closes or moves the current turn. What an earlier result of the same cycle had (its merged pull request, its passed build, its deploy, a merge you declined for it) says nothing about the current result. A turn the Node may have taken without its receipt reaching the Hub is checked again under the same operation, never sent a second time. An item without a session counts as unknown, not as settled, and a judge result counts only for the current version of the item's goal.
- A migration adds empty tables for these facts: delivery facts with their provenance, waits, judge results, and `work_policy`, which holds overrides of the named work thresholds. It also indexes the turns already run, which takes about 1.5 seconds per million turns. It runs by itself.

The 30-second stall watch now also sweeps every open item (not started, working and closing; archived items are skipped) and works out the one thing it would do to move each item on: wake the agent, raise a card for you, or remind you. This is observe-only. Every action has an execution switch, and all of them are off, so nothing new is sent, raised or changed. Today's stall card and Steve's follow-ups still do everything they did before.

- The decision is recorded once per item, cycle, session, turn, goal version, action, reason and the one thing it is about (an operation, a card or a wait), so a restart neither loses nor repeats it, and the same reason about another build or wait is a decision of its own. A decision that stops holding is marked cleared. If it comes back, it counts as a new episode of the same record.
- Every clock comes from the named work policy. With an open card, the sweep adds nothing.
- It names the stuck states nothing moves on today, each as a card it would raise:
  - a pull request problem the agent was told about and left on the same commit;
  - a merge you declined;
  - a closing waiting for this Hub to run the result;
  - a failed required build;
  - a wrap-up module that went silent;
  - the session's Node gone while closing;
  - an item paused when you dismissed a card, with nothing since;
  - a card you answered on a paused item.
- The item's Debug view shows, under "Derived state (shadow)", what the sweep would do, when it comes due, and its recent decisions. `GET /api/items/{id}` carries the same under `shadow.would_do`.
- A migration adds the empty table for this log. It runs by itself and is fast on a large database.
