# Steve v0.2.116

- When Steve decides not to answer a message, the message's signal now says so and why: a reply Steve read and chose not to write keeps "recorded" with the composer's reason ("起草器：…"), and a message only recorded by the rules says "规则" with the rule. A question to you opened later by the conversation path also shows on the signal as waiting for you.
- For contributors: an offline replay of owner-voice replies (`cargo run -p steve-channels --example reply_eval -- replay|baseline|check|judge`). It rebuilds the reply prompt for saved cases, asks the model Steve uses for channel replies without writing any Steve database or sending anything, and scores the answers: decision accuracy, recall on cases where Steve should act, skips while you are present, length and punctuation against your own messages, and leaks of Steve's own system sentences.

- On your own chat account, Steve no longer drafts a reply, or asks you about a message, once you have answered it yourself, or while you are chatting there (you wrote in it within the last 3 minutes and the message goes on with that: in a private chat always, in a group when it answers you, @-mentions you or follows you directly). In a group, what you write counts as an answer only when it is linked to the message: a reply to it (or to its sender's message in the same reply chain or run of messages), in its thread without replying to someone else, @-mentioning its sender, or its sender answering you right after (nobody in between). Just saying something in the group a minute later does not silence everyone else's messages. Steve checks again after drafting, before YOLO approves or retries the draft, and before it is sent; a message of yours that reached Steve late, or whose time is a few seconds ahead of the Hub's clock, still counts as soon as it is stored. A reply you approved yourself still goes out. An automatic reply another assistant posts on your account (an "[AI]" or "AI 代答" message) does not count as you.
- When you write in a chat yourself, the drafts and "找你" cards Steve still had waiting there about older messages you have now answered close as "你已亲自回复". Where a bot speaks for Steve, your messages leave its cards alone.
- Several messages in a row from the same person (each within a minute of the last, nobody else in between) are now answered once, together, and the cards for the earlier ones close. A message that already has a reply in your name approved or sent is left out of the next turn, and YOLO never approves or sends a draft whose message a later turn took over.
- A group or private chat on your own account has a new setting, "替你回复": 起草 (as before), 只记录 (nothing is drafted, asked or answered for you there and no rule or flow runs on other people's messages; a reply in an item's thread still reaches that item) or 不处理 (Steve leaves the chat alone on your account and only saves its messages; your `/bot` still works). Changing it away from 起草 closes the drafts and cards still open there, and YOLO approves and sends nothing more there unless you approve it yourself. It is on the chat's page under "Steve 在这里", and in `PATCH /spaces/:id` as `reply_as_you` (`draft` | `record` | `off`). It has no effect where a bot speaks.

- On your own chat account, saying nothing is now the default. Steve hands a message to you only when the reply would commit you to something (a time, money, deleting something, privacy, a relationship), or, when the message is to you, when it needs something only you know or is a long message worth reading yourself. Code checks this after the model answers; the phrase list that used to turn some replies into questions is gone.
- A message with nothing to answer (a sticker, emoji, a system message, an empty card) is recorded without asking any model, unless the sender's question is still waiting. Steve decides this by the message's shape: a forwarded chat, a task, a shared chat or a location is always read. A lone "？" or emoji while a card about the sender's earlier message is still open adds "对方又在催" to that card instead of opening another one.
- When a message still cannot be placed after Steve has read further back, it comes to you only if it asks you directly; otherwise it is let go. The card never says that Steve could not follow.
- The card that hands a message to you now shows one sentence on who wants what, why you are needed, the last few lines of the conversation with yours marked "我", and a reply you can send as it is when Steve could write one. Its choices: 发这条草稿, 告诉它怎么回 (your words go to the composer, and its reply comes back as a draft for your approval), 按原文发 (your words go out exactly as you typed them), 转事项 and 不用管. 按原文发 counts as your approval; it goes out after the usual short delay and can be undone within it. 发这条草稿 first checks the draft for anything that should not be said in that chat, like every reply: when it passes it goes out the same way; when it may leak, you get the usual approval with the suspicious parts marked. YOLO never makes these choices.
- A card about your own account closes when you speak in that chat, or when a newer card about the same person or reply chain shows its message. A card asking you to commit to something is only closed by another such card, and a starred contact's only by another starred one. Cards do not expire on time unless you set a lifetime on the account (`card_ttl_h` on `PATCH /channels/:id`, 1 hour to 30 days); even then, commitments and starred contacts' cards wait for you.
- Messages from a contact you starred on the contact page always reach you as a card marked "⭐ 星标联系人", even where Steve does not reply for you, unless you already answered or are in that conversation.
- A bot's message on your own account becomes a proposed item by its structure, not its words: in a private chat with the bot, a notice that carries an action link. A new setting on that chat's page, "机器人的通知", can turn this into every message of that bot (each one once) or record only.
- YOLO looks at a card's stage before its send option, so a hand-off card says it is yours rather than that it has no send option. On your own account YOLO approves only a draft the composer wrote, and never one that would promise something for you; such a draft says so on its card.
- On a bot's account nothing changes: its prompt and its approval rule are as before.

- Drafts on your own chat account are written as you, not as the Bot behind the account. The Bot now only picks the model there; its name, duty, manner and working rules are no longer in the prompt, and neither are the item numbers of the person who wrote.
- The composer is shown a few messages you typed yourself on that account: from the same chat first, then from chats of the same kind, most recent first. Links, attachments, placeholders, redactions and anything shaped like a key are left out. A message from another chat is used only when it is short and has no digits, no @ and no name of anyone in your chats, so nothing said in one chat reaches another. It also gets your habits in this kind of situation (a private chat, a group where someone @-ed or answered you, a group): how long your messages usually are, how often they end in a full stop or ask something, how you open them and which emoji you use. These are computed from your recent messages each time a reply is drafted, so they follow how you talk.
- Drafts you edited before sending are shown, as you sent them, as "like this". A draft you rejected is shown as "not like this" only when you had not said anything yourself in that chat from shortly before the message until you rejected it, and the message was not merged into a later one: most rejected drafts sounded like you and were wrong only because you had already answered. A draft approved unchanged or by YOLO is not used.
- The rules of writing as you: one or two short lines, no "收到 / 好的" openers, no promises on your behalf (those come to you instead), at most one question and only when needed, no options to pick from, no mention of Steve, items or queues, nothing that contradicts what you already said, and when there is nothing solid to say, something short or nothing.
- After a draft is written as you, it is checked: a list of options, more than one question, a draft more than twice as long as your usual long messages in that situation, or an opener you never use. A draft that fails goes back once with what was wrong; if the second one still fails, nothing is drafted and the message comes to you as a question with what was wrong, so it is never dropped. Replies on bot accounts are not checked this way, and neither is anything you said to write yourself: your trigger words, a flow step's text, or your answer in a conversation.

- When Steve drafts a chat reply, it now reads the conversation the way you do. Your own lines show as `<你的名字>（我）` and what Steve sent for you as `（我，经 Steve 发出）`. Steve tells your lines apart by your account and contact, not by name, so an account whose display name is a number still reads as you. All your names are listed once, and @-ing any of them means someone is looking for you. Numeric ids and raw handles never appear as names.
- Private chats and groups are both read in stretches of talk: the stretch the message belongs to and the one before it. A gap of half an hour starts a new stretch, and the gap shows. Every line has a relative time ("3 分钟前", "昨天 21:40"). A line Steve sent for you is placed at the time it was sent, not when it was drafted, so a draft you approved later sits where it appeared in the chat. Lines in the same Feishu reply chain as the message are marked, and earlier lines of that chain are added when they fall outside the stretches.
- A new 【场合】 block tells the composer whether this is a private chat or a group (with its member count and whether outsiders are in it), why the message reached Steve (a private chat, an @, a reply to you, your trigger words), and the local time. Messages that arrived after the one being answered are shown and marked, so Steve does not answer a question that has already moved on. Drafts still waiting in the chat are listed so they are not written twice.
- Runs of bot messages fold into one line. Placeholders such as `[unsupported message]`, `[system message]` or `[sticker]` become plain descriptions. A message with only an attachment is kept. When Steve has read an image in a message, a one-line description is kept with it, and later conversations show that description instead of "unread". Steve is told never to guess what is in an image, card or voice message it could not read.
- Your own account takes your name when you speak, if its display name was an id (from another account of yours, else your contact's name).
- Feishu rich text keeps the text of tags Steve does not know, instead of dropping them. On an external chat connection, a message whose text is only a placeholder is read again from the Feishu message in its `raw` payload, when that payload carries one, so rich text and cards keep their words.
- On your own account, a message nobody can read (a voice message, forwarded chat history, a card) that is still not understood after the wider read is recorded, not asked about: you see it in the chat yourself. On a bot it still comes to you as a question, because whoever asked the bot may be in a chat you are not in.

- On your own chat account, only the reply composer speaks as you. A message there no longer goes through Steve's conversation router. Its clarifying questions (with "（可以回复：…）" options) and its "前面还有 3 个问题在排队…" can no longer turn up as drafts in your name. The composer decides whether to reply, skip, or ask you first.
- What the composer decided about a message (reply, skip or ask, whether it needs your approval, and why) is now kept with the message. It survives a Hub restart. Before, a reply whose decision had been lost could skip those checks.
- Steve's fixed receipts ("收到，已记为 …", "我请 … 来看。", "… 已完成") are no longer drafted in your name. Steve no longer puts reactions on other people's messages as you. The item, the handoff and the report still reach you in Steve.
- Your trigger words on your own account (`/ai`, `/bot`) still answer as you with the `[AI]` mark as their settings say, but only with what the composer wrote for that turn: the router's questions and queue messages are never sent instead.
- Steve's own status messages (queue full, stopped, errors) are never said in a chat, not even by a bot. If a message got only "queue full" as an answer, it now reaches you as a handoff.

Assistant replies now keep permitted local Markdown images, including temporary QR images, in the Hub's authenticated attachment store. New images remain visible after the execution Node removes its source, the conversation is refreshed or the Node disconnects. Image capture validates execution scope, GFM image syntax, raster content and transfer integrity; malformed images cannot exhaust the budget for subsequent uploads. Failed captures preserve the text response.

Conversation images load through the currently connected Hub with the image's original workspace. Loading failures show a localized explanation and a retry button, including on narrow screens. Delivery-package relative images keep their existing behavior.

Tools should write into their execution directory or honor its `TMPDIR`. Existing missing or expired authorization QR images are not recovered; regenerate them when needed. No public machine-path reader or automatic attachment garbage collection is added. Older peers remain compatible and leave unsupported captures unchanged.
