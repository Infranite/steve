# Steve v0.2.18

Steve now knows, every time it answers in a chat, who is asking and who else reads the answer.

- Each answer in a Feishu chat starts from a conversation card built from Steve's records:
  - where it is;
  - who asks and what they are to you (you, a colleague, someone from another company, or unknown);
  - who else in the chat will read the answer;
  - how many members Steve has no record of;
  - the identity it speaks as;
  - whether this person can start work or only propose it.

  Nothing on the card is guessed: a person Steve has no record of is "unknown" and is treated as an outsider. The card is information for Steve, while its tools still check every request on their own.
- A chat counts as "everyone here is one of us" only when Steve has a record of every member. Before, it was enough that everyone it had a record of was a workspace user. Members it has no record of could be anyone, so they no longer widen what Steve may say there.

No database migration (schema 30). Hub and Web must come from the same release.
