# Steve v0.2.37

Estimated spend now prices models automatically from [Models.dev](https://models.dev) (the open, MIT-licensed model catalog OpenCode uses), so a model only shows as unpriced when no price is known anywhere. (ST-52)

- The Hub syncs `https://models.dev/api.json` shortly after it starts and then once a day, and keeps the prices in its database. If a sync fails, it keeps using the last prices and shows when and why it failed. 设置 · 统计 has a 立即同步 button. `STEVE_PRICE_CATALOG_URL` points the sync at a mirror; `off` turns it off.
- Models are matched by provider and model id, never by display name alone. `provider/model` names its provider; otherwise the id's lab is used (`claude-…` → Anthropic, `gpt-…`/`o3` → OpenAI, `gemini-…`, `glm-…`, `kimi-…`, `deepseek-…`, `qwen…` …). A trailing release date is treated as an alias, and `kiro/<model>` is still priced as `<model>` and marked as a rough estimate.
- The price table still wins: its rows (or the built-in official prices) override Models.dev, so proxied, self-hosted or discounted models can keep their own price. Prices are in USD per million tokens, the same unit as Models.dev. When Models.dev lists no cache-read or cache-write price, those tokens are priced at the input price instead of being treated as free. For tiered or long-context prices, only the base price is used.
- Past estimates stay put. A Models.dev price change applies from the day after the sync that saw it, and a day's cost always uses the price in force on that day. Editing the price table still recalculates all history, as before.
- The stats page says "费用未完整估算" and how many tokens were left out when a model has no price, names where the unit prices came from (price table, Models.dev and its last sync), and links to 管理价格. 设置 · 统计 lists every model used with its price source and unit prices; 改价 copies a Models.dev price into the price table as an override.
- Database migration 32 adds `price_catalog` and `price_sync`.
