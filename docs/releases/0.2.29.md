# Steve v0.2.29

The YOLO bar at the top of every page is gone; its status and buttons now open the stats page, which leads with what matters and draws its trends instead of listing numbers.

- The mode, "这次替你做了 / 留给你" and the YOLO 记录 / 调整 / 恢复 buttons are in a "运行状态" line at the top of the stats page; the counts open the YOLO record on the matching filter. When YOLO is paused, the inbox shows the same line with 恢复, and phones see the YOLO dot on the inbox. (ST-40)
- The stats page starts with four figures: 进行中, 待你处理, 已完成 and 失败或受阻. Each one opens the list of items behind it. Next come created vs. done per day as bars (a day opens its items), and the unfinished items by status (a status opens its items). Below are lead time as a median line with a p85 band, where the time went, usage and cost, quality, and the item table.
- Every chart names its unit and date range. A status keeps one colour across the page, matching the item list's dots. Groupings such as model, Bot or project use a separate set of five colours, checked for colour-blind separation in light and dark, and from the sixth group on they fold into "其他". Legends carry the totals, and every trend can switch to a table.
- `GET /api/stats/items?only=done|created` lists exactly the items behind the summary's done and created counts.
