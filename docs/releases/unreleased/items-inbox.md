- The item page leads with one state: what is happening and whose move it is. It sits in a "now" card that holds the decisions, blockers and workflow actions, and says why the item cannot finish yet. The items list, board, inbox, chat cards, contact and team pages, sessions, stats and Bot pages all show this same state, so an item whose move is yours is under 等你 everywhere. A work graph from an earlier cycle no longer changes the item's state.
- On the item page, a long goal folds to its first three lines. Runs of process notes fold to one line without Markdown marks. The activity is grouped under day headings with the clock time on each line. Progress and properties sit in a side column, with rarely used properties folded.
- Collaboration on the item page is one list with a row per step: its Bot and model, what it is doing now, on which try it passed, why it failed or what it asks you, a progress bar, and its working time and cost, earlier tries included. Each row has one action: 去处理 (decide), 重试 (retry) or 打开 (open). Steps that need you, failed or are running always show. Of the finished steps only the latest three show, the rest behind "显示其余 N 步". The header counts what needs you, failed, runs and is done, and shows the elapsed time against the agents' working time and the estimated cost. When some tokens ran on models without a price, it says so instead of showing a near-zero cost. Selecting a row opens its detail beside the page, with its earlier tries.
- "查看全图" opens the whole collaboration as one dependency graph of its steps. Each card shows the step's Bot, title, outcome, tries, working time and cost. Steps that could run at the same time share a column. Select a step for its goal and report, outputs, checks, failure reason, earlier tries, neighbouring steps, retry or rework, and, on request, its tool calls on a timeline.
- The items page has three views: list, board and the new 协作 view. In 协作, each open item that coordinates steps is a card, and its steps hang beside it with their state, cost and time. The 关系图 view of all items is gone, because it grew into a tangle. An open item's row now names what it waits for ("等 ST-28"), and an item's own relation graph still opens from its menu.
- The board has four columns, sorted the way the list groups items: "Your move", "In progress", "Paused / blocked" and "Done". An empty column keeps its place and says so. Each card shows the number, the title, its state in words and plan progress. Its arrow opens the item, and clicking the card unfolds its latest steps. Done shows the latest eight until "Show all". The counts under the title, the filters, the board and the running-status pill always agree.
- Items closed as failed have their own Failed group in the list, count as closed on project pages, read Failed on conversation cards and can be reopened.
- Item goals that Steve writes, and the briefs a coordinator writes for its steps, are short Markdown: one sentence on what will be done and what counts as done, then only the sections that apply.
- In 待处理 (Inbox), the 已处理 tab reads back the last 7 days by day and by item. Open an item to see each decision, who made it and, while possible, an undo. Filters separate your decisions, the ones YOLO made for you and the ones that lapsed. An item whose collaboration cannot run (its Node is unavailable, a Bot is off) counts as waiting on you. It is listed under 无法继续 with the reason, and the item states that reason instead of "协作已暂停".
- A work graph that its coordinator paused without asking you anything now waits for you: Steve opens a card with the coordinator's reason, and the item moves to Waiting for you instead of sitting under Paused. A graph you paused yourself stays quiet.
