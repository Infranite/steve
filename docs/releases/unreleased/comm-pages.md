- Chats opens with what needs you. If an account stopped working or a new group has not been set up, a notice at the top names it and links to it. Each group or DM row now says what Steve does there, for example "Steve replies directly", "Drafts for you, you approve" or "Quiet: record only", with the time of the last message on the right. The provider and member count are no longer repeated on every row. One account filter replaces the separate source and account filters. Account rows show their provider, whom they speak as and how many chats they cover, and show a status only when something is wrong.
- A group's page starts with Steve's part there. The attention setting, role, record-only switch, persona and speaking identity sit in one card. If replies are waiting for your approval, a notice says how many and links to the inbox. The latest five messages come next, with "All N" for the rest. Duty and response rules follow. Knowledge and memory, item defaults, collaboration with other bots and members are folded under "More settings". The two persona rows are now one, and the speaking identity only shows when there is more than one to choose from.
- An account's page lists its groups and DMs first, each with what Steve does there and its duty on one line. A broken connection is a notice at the top with the error and a re-authorize button, and Check sits next to "Account and authorization". The default persona, rules and undo window are one section. Trigger words show only their switch until you turn them on. After a permission check, only missing permissions are listed. Granted ones fold into one line, and each scope ID shows inline instead of behind its own disclosure.
- A persona's page says where the persona is used at the top, instead of repeating its introduction, and lists those groups before the form.
- Connections: when something needs attention, a notice at the top names the service, the app or mailbox, and what is wrong. Only the problem part of a card's line is highlighted. With a handful of services, the page shows them without search, filter or categories. A service that is not connected shows how to connect first, then what it can do. Planned capabilities are one line, and the empty "Connected · 0" box is gone. A connected service shows its installations first, with "Connect another" folded below. The Feishu "authorize yourself" card is hidden once you have done so, and an identity repeats its status only when it differs from the card's.
- Mail: the mailbox filter is a menu next to the search, so the list has one row of controls. A draft waiting for your approval comes first on a thread row. On a thread, "Done" is a button in the header instead of a menu item.
- The wording is now "connect" everywhere on these pages: Connections, the mail and Feishu dialogs, and the automation guide. Chat pages say "account" instead of "channel account".
- Signals: the steps under "How it was handled" show each node's title instead of its ID. On a phone, a flow that failed today shows the count under its description instead of in a pill beside it.
