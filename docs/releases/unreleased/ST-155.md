Assistant replies now keep permitted local Markdown images, including temporary QR images, in the Hub's authenticated attachment store. New images remain visible after the execution Node removes its source, the conversation is refreshed or the Node disconnects. Image capture validates execution scope, GFM image syntax, raster content and transfer integrity; malformed images cannot exhaust the budget for subsequent uploads. Failed captures preserve the text response.

Conversation images load through the currently connected Hub with the image's original workspace. Loading failures show a localized explanation and a retry button, including on narrow screens. Delivery-package relative images keep their existing behavior.

Tools should write into their execution directory or honor its `TMPDIR`. Existing missing or expired authorization QR images are not recovered; regenerate them when needed. No public machine-path reader or automatic attachment garbage collection is added. Older peers remain compatible and leave unsupported captures unchanged.
