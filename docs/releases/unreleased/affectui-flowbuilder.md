- The flow editor (Automation → a flow) is now a canvas you build on. The flow runs from top to bottom as cards that say what each step does, for example its condition, address, command or question, with its branches as named outputs. Add node opens a list of steps (it starts open on a new flow): click one to add it under the selected step, drag it onto the canvas, or use the + under a free branch. A step dropped while another is selected joins after it. To connect two steps, drag from an output to another step. To remove a connection, select it and press Delete. The canvas won't make a loop, and ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z) takes back the last additions, connections and removals made on it. A step that nothing leads to from the trigger is marked Not connected and listed above the canvas, and Save waits until it is joined in, since the Hub refuses such a flow. Clicking a step opens its settings beside the canvas, or as a sheet on a phone. These are the same settings as before. The canvas pans, zooms and fits the flow, and it can be used with the keyboard alone. A step you drag keeps its place on that device. The flow itself has no positions, so when Steve edits it or you roll it back the whole flow is laid out again.
- Dry run now sits on the canvas. It replays the finished run step by step along the path it took, labelled as a replay, and the steps it skipped fade back. A run panel under the canvas lists the flow's recent runs: open one to replay its path, or see its steps, timeline, the event it used and what failed. On a phone the panel takes half the canvas and the page brings it into view. These used to be lists below the editor. Saving, versions, Steve's edits and the code view are unchanged.
