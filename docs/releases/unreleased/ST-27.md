The collaboration area now separates overview, current work and delivery history. It shows who is waiting on whom, links to child items and sessions, and keeps earlier attempts available for review without counting them toward a new cycle. Running units can be stopped individually; a completed unit offers a rework impact preview before its results and downstream evidence are reset. Execution is shown as confirmed only while the Hub has fresh activity from the exact active Node attempt; otherwise it is labeled active but unconfirmed.

The relation graph now has separate dependency and provenance views. Ownership links are visually distinct from blockers, missing references stay visible, and narrow screens show an equivalent list. Select a node to inspect it, then use the explicit open action to enter its item or conversation.
