- An item's statistics page shows where its cost went. Every turn of the item is one step, and the items it started (for example the steps of a Bot collaboration) are folded groups with their own turns, all in the order they began. Each step's bar starts where the one before it ended, so the bars add up to the total and the expensive steps are the long ones. A switch measures the same steps in tokens instead, and the most expensive step is named on top, together with the item it belongs to when it is a turn of one of the started items. Tokens without a price are not counted in the cost: a step that has some shows a mark next to its cost, the total says how many tokens it leaves out, and the step's details show the adapter's own quote when there is one. While the item is running, the started items' figures keep up with it. Opening a step shows its start, duration, stop reason, tools, token split and estimated cost, with a link to its conversation; a folded item links to its own page and statistics. This replaces the list of turns on that page.
- The usage table on the statistics page has the split of the range's estimated cost next to it, for the dimension you picked (model, Bot, project or purpose): the total, a bar of the parts and the parts ranked with their share. When nothing in the range has a price, it splits the tokens instead and says so. It names at most five parts, each in its own colour, and folds the rest into one. The change against the previous range is shown in a neutral colour, the same figure as the estimated-cost card. The share column of the table went, since the split now shows it.
