What Steve says in a chat for a conversation message now goes out once, even when its delivery runs twice: two delivery passes at the same time, or a second one after a restart that came between the send and its record. This covers the short note sent when a turn failed (your trigger word's note as you, a bot's “我请 … 来看”), replies, item receipts and trigger-word answers. A repeated delivery finds the message already stored for that turn and reports its state instead of creating and sending another one; every retry of it carries the same dedupe key to the provider.

When the provider may have taken a message but its answer was lost, the message stays `unknown` and is not sent again from the conversation. Only the send queue retries it, under the same dedupe key within the provider's dedupe window, and otherwise asks you. The conversation now shows such a message as `unknown`, not as pending.
