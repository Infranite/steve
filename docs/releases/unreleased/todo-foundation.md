- Todos (我的待办) are now every member's own, not only the workspace owner's: each member lists, adds, changes and undoes their own todos through `/api/digest/todos`, and nobody sees another member's. The matter page shows only your own todos. Only the workspace owner's todos link to matters, source messages and people, which come from the owner's digest; another member's todo refuses them and never shows their content.
- A todo can be a **goal** with a target date and todos under it (one level), and items can be linked to a goal. A goal shows its progress: done out of everything not dropped, counting a todo handed to a Bot as its item and each item once; with nothing under it yet it shows no percentage.
- A deadline is either a moment or just a day; a day-only deadline keeps its day in every time zone. Todos also hold a reminder time, a snooze time, a priority (0–3), a star, and a repeat rule (daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, every N): completing a repeating todo creates the next one, and undoing the completion takes it back. Reminders are stored but do not notify yet.
- Agents can work with your todos: `steve_todo_list`, `steve_todo_add`, `steve_todo_update` and `steve_todo_complete` (find them with `steve_tools_search`). They only act for the person whose session it is and refuse a session that runs for the workspace; they change only your own todos, at once and undoably, and contact nobody. Dates must be written out (`YYYY-MM-DD` or a time with its offset) and are echoed back.
- The web app no longer reloads its whole state on every digest change; pages that show digest data refresh themselves.
