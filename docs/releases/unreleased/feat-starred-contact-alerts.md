- A starred contact's message to you on your own chat account now also reaches you as a personal notification, sent by your notification settings ("通知") to your private chat with the bot you picked. It covers a private chat, an @ of you, or a reply to you in a group. It is sent whatever the chat's replies are set to: the rules, 只记录 (静默), "只记录：不替你回复", a chat where no identity of yours speaks, or an account without the grant to send as you. A chat set to 不处理, 不提醒 or 屏蔽 still keeps it out. The star never makes a draft. A draft or card still comes only from the rules and the drafter, as before.
- In a group where both your own account and a Steve bot are members, the message is checked for you whichever copy arrives first. Your replies in either copy count as answering it.
- Purging a message's text or deleting it (清理记录, retention) also purges the notification's copy of it, the message sent to you and its delivery content. A notification not sent yet is not sent.
- Nothing is sent once you have answered yourself. Steve checks this when the message comes in, again before the notification is sent, and again before any retry. If you replied in the meantime, the notification is cancelled. Follow-up messages from the same person are notified once, until you speak in that chat again.
- A starred contact's message is the urgent kind. Under "摘要" it is still sent right away, but only to the bot conversation you choose for it on the settings page; with none chosen it stays in your feed. Under "记录" it only goes to your feed. A send cut off before it began (a restart, maintenance) is picked up again under "摘要" too. Quiet hours hold it until they end, unless you turn on the new switch "星标联系人不受免打扰限制". The feed shows these notifications as their own kind, "星标联系人", with a filter for them.
- The star on a contact's page is now called "设为星标联系人". When set, the page explains what it does. The star is set in Steve only and is not synced with Feishu/Lark starred contacts: Feishu's Open API offers no way to read a user's starred contacts.
- The database moves to migration 101. It adds the notification's kind and message, the quiet-hours switch, the feed kind, and the triggers that purge a notification's copy with its message. The feed table is rebuilt in place; its entries, read marks and numbering are kept.
