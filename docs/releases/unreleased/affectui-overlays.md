- Dialogs, confirmations and side sheets across Steve take affectUI's look: a softer dimming behind them, one card with a soft edge, a quieter title and description, a lighter close ×, and a short rise as they open and a fade as they close. On a phone, or in a narrow window, a dialog or confirmation slides up from the bottom as a sheet across the full width, with its buttons in reach of your thumb. Search (⌘K) still opens near the top. What each dialog asks and does is unchanged.
- Menus (the ⋯ menus, the workspace and Hub switchers, tag and dependency pickers) show their rows in a quieter colour that darkens under the pointer or the arrow keys, with one soft highlight gliding from row to row (moving with the arrow keys no longer draws a focus ring on top of it); red items such as Drop and Delete keep a red highlight. Keyboard use, type-ahead and the menus' contents are unchanged.
- Popovers (the account menu, the model and owner pickers, "send to which item", the custom date range on Statistics, resuming a session) and tooltips are affectUI's. A tooltip shows after a short rest of the pointer and at once under keyboard focus, and once one is showing, its neighbours show without waiting. Popovers flip to whichever side has room and close on a click outside, Escape or tabbing away.
- Toasts are affectUI's: they stack with the newest in front and fan out when you point at them, can be swiped away or dismissed with ×, and pause while you point at them; F8 moves the focus to the newest. They now appear just above the composer, centred over it, so they no longer cover the Send or Stop button; on a phone they sit above the composer or the tab bar. While a dialog or sheet is open, a toast from it appears above or beside the dialog instead of on top of its fields; when the dialog fills the window, the toast goes to the top edge.
