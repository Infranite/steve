- Search (⌘K) is a new command menu. With nothing typed it lists actions and pages first, then items, sessions, projects and Bots. With text typed, what it found comes first: items and sessions by their title or number (an item whose number you typed comes first, then the most recently changed), sessions whose transcript mentions the text (marked as found in the transcript), messages of the main conversation that mention it (selecting one jumps to it), and contacts and teams. ↑ and ↓ move, Tab jumps between groups, Enter runs, and Esc clears the text before it closes the menu. A spinner shows while the Hub searches, and the menu says when nothing matches. The "N" key hint next to "New item" is gone, because no such shortcut existed.
- Settings › Activity log lists each recorded action as a sentence, grouped by day with a count. A coloured dot shows who did it: you, a Bot on its own, YOLO deciding for you, another member, or the Hub itself. The Hub's own upkeep, such as a migration, an expiry or a seeded default, counts as the Hub's even though it is recorded under Steve's name. Buttons above the list filter by who, with counts, and a search field (press /) filters by words. Both cover the entries loaded so far. Opening an entry shows when, who, the action and what changed, plus, for a YOLO decision, that YOLO answered for you, and a link to its item when it has one. Repeated identical actions still fold into one row, which opens into the individual steps, each showing what it was done to. J and K move between entries.
