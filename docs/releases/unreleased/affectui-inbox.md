- The items board has four columns that sort items the same way the list groups them: "Your move" (a decision or proposal waiting on you, or a collaboration that cannot run until you fix it), "In progress" (the agents are working, your message is queued, or the item is open with nothing asked), "Paused / blocked" (a paused item or collaboration, or one waiting for its prerequisites) and "Done". An empty column keeps its place and width and says it is empty. The counts under the page title, the "Waiting on you" and "In progress" filters, the board and the running-status pill now always agree.
- Each card shows the item's number, its title on up to two lines, what it is on, its state in words and last update, and how far its plan is. On a narrow column the card keeps the state word and leaves out the time. The arrow in a card's corner opens the item in one click; clicking the card unfolds the plan's last steps, the project, due date and tags. The Done column shows the latest eight until you choose "Show all". Failed and dropped items stay off the board, as before.
- A running-status pill sits under the workspace switcher in the sidebar, and in the page header on a phone: "1 in progress · 2 waiting on you". Open it to see the open items with the step each one is on and its plan progress, pause or resume an item, or open one that waits on you or whose collaboration is paused. It replaces the in-progress count beside "Items" in the sidebar.
- The board and the pill are built on affectUI Pro components (Agent Inbox and Running Now), styled with Steve's own colors, type and motion in light and dark mode.
