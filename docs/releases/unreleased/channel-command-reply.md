Trigger words (now `/ai` by default, besides `/bot`) work only on messages your own account receives; what a bot receives keeps the bot's own rules. The answer comes from your account in that chat, starting with `[AI]`, which the sending step adds, never the model. Where it goes is a setting: in the message's thread (the default; a quote reply in private chats), as a quote reply, or as a plain message. Once your account has left the chat, nothing is answered. A new setting says who may use the words: only you (the default), anyone, or chosen contacts. When someone else asks for your personal details, the deployment or Steve itself, the request is politely declined and you get a notification. When it can't be told, the reply says to wait for you and you are notified. The conversation gets a dedicated reply skill. It answers from the chat's context or hands the work to an item. Such an item reports key progress back to the message, says when it is done there, and then the message's reaction turns to done.
