Trigger words (now `/ai` by default, besides `/bot`) work only on messages your own account receives; what a bot receives keeps the bot's own rules. The answer comes from your account in that chat, starting with `[AI]`, which the sending step adds, never the model. Where it goes is a setting: in the message's thread (the default; a quote reply in private chats), as a quote reply, or as a plain message. Nothing is answered once your account has left the chat. A conversation goes on without the word: your next message in the same thread, or a reply to the answer, is taken too and answered there. If the first one became an item, it goes to that item.

A new setting says who may use the words: only you (the default), anyone, or chosen contacts. When someone else asks for your personal details, the deployment or Steve itself, the request is politely declined and you get a notification. When it can't be told, the reply says to wait for you and you are notified.

Only the instruction decides whether a trigger word is answered or handed off as work, so a plain question gets an answer instead of an item. The answer knows the current date and time. Work handed off from your own trigger word is requested by you and runs as you. While it runs, a failure is reported in the thread straight away (at most every half hour for the same failure), and progress every half hour when it changed. When it ends, nothing is said: the message's reaction turns to done.
