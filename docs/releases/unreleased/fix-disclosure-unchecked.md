- A disclosure check whose model gave no answer (it timed out, failed or answered something unreadable) is no longer shown as a possible leak. The reply's basis says `unchecked` and names every model tried, and the card says "检查没跑完：…" with what happened instead of "可能泄露". Such a reply is still never sent without you: it waits for your approval, and YOLO does not send it ("披露检查没跑完，不自动发"). A GitHub comment whose check did not finish is not posted under a grant either.
- On your own chat account a draft no longer waits for the check model before its card opens. The card opens at once with "（披露检查还在跑）", the check runs afterwards (up to 60 seconds, and once more on the gateway's smart tier if the first try fails) and the card then shows what it found. YOLO waits for the result. If you approve or discard the draft first, its basis says the check did not finish.
- The check now reads the conversation the people in the chat can already see, not only the message being answered. It also knows the samples of how you talk that the drafter was shown: copying their content into a reply is flagged, and their tone is not.
- A rewrite of a flagged reply may only delete the flagged parts. A rewrite that adds words is thrown away and the original stays as it was, flagged, for you to look at. So is a rewrite that leaves only hedges such as "这个还不好说": it is not sent in place of the reply. Only when the rewrite leaves no text at all is there nothing to say: the reply is dropped, its card closes saying why, and a bot reply dropped this way is recorded in the audit log. A rewrite that leaves a plain answer such as "没有" or "确定" is an answer and is checked again like any other.
