- The work behind an answer reads as one timeline, in Steve's conversation and in an agent's run alike. Each step sits on a line down the left: a spinner while it runs, a dot once it is done, a check when the work is finished. A step's verb follows its state ("Reading" becomes "Read 5 files"); calls of one kind in a row merge into one line with their files, queries or commands as chips, opened for each call. Thinking streams in while it runs, with a clock, and folds to "Thought for 6s" after. A plan shows its steps in a small card that checks them off, and handing work to a subagent reads "Delegated to …".
- Steve's own tools in an agent's run go by what they do ("Read item", "Search knowledge", "Diagnose") instead of their internal names, and several reads in a row merge into one line.
