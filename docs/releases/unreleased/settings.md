- Settings → Appearance has colours and type: seven accents (Indigo, Blue, Violet, Green, Orange, Rose, Graphite), three neutrals (Slate, Gray, Sand), the text and monospace fonts and three corner radii, with a live preview. Each choice shows at once, works in light and dark, and is kept on this device. Themes are JSON manifests over a whitelist of design tokens, the built-ins included, and `registerTheme` adds one after checking it. The format is in `docs/deployment.md`, "Appearance and themes".
- Font size and Reduce motion now take effect. Large makes all text about an eighth bigger without changing the layout. Reduce motion stops transitions, animations and smooth scrolling, as the system's own setting does. Both are stored on the Hub and apply in every browser you sign in from.
- The settings pages use one set of controls, and each option shows its full explanation. Workspace settings save on their own when you leave a field, so there is no save bar. A failed save keeps your text and marks it as not saved, and Escape puts a field back. In Notifications, each message is a switch, and turning on the daily summary or quiet hours unfolds its time. Clearing all Bot memories moved to a danger zone at the bottom of Data and privacy. Its confirmation opens in place with the focus on Cancel.
- Settings → Activity log lists each recorded action as a sentence, grouped by day. A coloured dot shows who did it: you, a Bot on its own, YOLO deciding for you, another member, or the Hub itself (its own upkeep, such as a migration, an expiry or a seeded default). Filter by who, or search with /. Open an entry to see what changed and, where there is one, its item. J and K move between entries. Actions that used to show as raw codes, such as reopened and dropped items, plan changes, decisions, proposed memories and cancelled flow runs, are named.
- The YOLO control ("How much Steve can do while you're away") is a three-stop dial (Off, YOLO, Full access). As you move it, it lists what each level handles for you and what still asks you first, with the preview of open cards right under it.
