On the stats page, "今天" (or any single picked day) now charts its trends hour by hour instead of as one bar.

- Created vs. done, lead time and the token, cost and hit-rate trends show the 24 local hours, 00:00–23:00, in the zone statistics use, named under each chart (for example "北京时间"). The figures at the top and the legend totals still show the whole day so far. (ST-43)
- The current hour is shaded and marked "未结束", and its tooltip says "截至 HH:MM". Hours still to come stay empty rather than showing 0, read "还没到这个小时" in the tooltip and "未到" in the table view, and can't be clicked. The page refreshes every quarter hour, so the current hour moves on by itself.
- Clicking an hour opens the items completed or created in exactly that hour (on the usage trend, the usage table for that hour). The tooltip and the list title name the hour, as in "11月5日 09:00–10:00". The hours of a day add up to the day's totals, and events at the first and last millisecond of a day stay on their own side of midnight.
- API: `GET /api/stats/series?bucket=hour` works for a one-day range and returns `spans`, `current` and `tz`. `GET /api/stats/items` and `GET /api/stats/breakdown` (token dimensions) take `hour=0..23`. Summaries name the actual zone instead of `local`.
- A version check started right after another one no longer fails with "版本检查正在进行" just because the Hub was starting a process at that moment. It now waits up to 2 seconds for the other check to finish.
