- On your own chat account, only the reply composer speaks as you. A message there no longer goes through Steve's conversation router. Its clarifying questions (with "（可以回复：…）" options) and its "前面还有 3 个问题在排队…" can no longer turn up as drafts in your name. The composer decides whether to reply, skip, or ask you first.
- What the composer decided about a message (reply, skip or ask, whether it needs your approval, and why) is now kept with the message. It survives a Hub restart. Before, a reply whose decision had been lost could skip those checks.
- Steve's fixed receipts ("收到，已记为 …", "我请 … 来看。", "… 已完成") are no longer drafted in your name. Steve no longer puts reactions on other people's messages as you. The item, the handoff and the report still reach you in Steve.
- Your `/bot` on your own account now always makes a draft for your approval. It used to send directly. Steve still writes it from your instruction, and once approved it goes out with the "由 Steve 代发" mark as before.
- Steve's own status messages (queue full, stopped, errors) are never said in a chat, not even by a bot. If a message got only "queue full" as an answer, it now reaches you as a handoff.
