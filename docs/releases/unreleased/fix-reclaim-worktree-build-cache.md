A closed item now gives back its toolchains' build outputs in `.steve/build` (Rust's `target` among them, often tens of GB) along with its other caches, also when its worktree stays because the branch is not merged yet. Before, only caches with a known name or a `CACHEDIR.TAG` were found, so a moved Cargo `target` stayed on disk after the item ended. Worktrees already closed are cleared by the next workspace pass; sources, commits and branches stay.
