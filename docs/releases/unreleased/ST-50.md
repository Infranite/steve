- **The conversation page is one continuous conversation with one compact bar.** The "你和 Steve / 会话列表" row is gone. The bar holds Steve's name, what he is doing right now (空闲, 正在想, 正在查, 正在回复), and two buttons. 历史 opens the history. 事项 opens the rail, where you see how many items are running and an orange dot when something waits on you; the count no longer sits under Steve's name. Group chats and DMs are listed from 历史.
- **History: search, topics, jump and back.** 历史 searches everything said in the conversation, including item numbers like ST-12, and highlights the match. Left empty, it lists the conversation by day and topic (a pause of 30 minutes starts a new one). Picking a result loads what lies between, centres the message and highlights it. A bar then offers "返回刚才位置" and "回到最新". Scrolling to the top loads older messages without moving what you are reading. Quotes and "看最新进展" jump the same way. New endpoints: `GET /api/thread/history` and `GET /api/thread/search`.
- **The page reopens where you were.** After a reload you return to the message you were reading, with the draft kept. If you were at the bottom, you stay at the bottom. While you read history, new messages don't move the page; the button counts them ("2 条新消息").
- **No light seam on a sideways swipe.** The conversation and the page no longer scroll or bounce sideways.
