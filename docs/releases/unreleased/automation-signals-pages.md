- The Automation page no longer opens on a strip of four counters. The line under the title shows today's signals, how many need you and how many failed, and links to Signals. When something on another tab needs you, a notice above the list names it: a flow that failed today, a schedule whose last run failed, or an account that must be authorized again.
- With no flows and no schedules yet, the Flows tab shows three steps to start: connect an account, create a flow, create a schedule. The first step is ticked once an account is connected.
- Each flow takes one line. It shows the flow's description, or its trigger as the source and event names instead of event keys such as `github.check_failed`. On the right is when it last ran, or how many events it failed on today. Flows that are on come first. The internal hosts flows may reach are now a folded line under the list that shows the current hosts.
- Schedules show their time and next run in one line. A failed last run is a "Last run failed" pill instead of the whole error, and the Run now button and Bot avatar are no longer on every row. Opening a schedule shows the error first, cut to two lines with "Show all". Below it are what the schedule does, when it runs, who runs it, and its rules on one line. In the run list, click a failed run to read its error. In the New schedule dialog, the options for missed and overlapping runs moved under Advanced, next to cron and time zone.
- Event sources list connected ones first. A source shows its accounts and events in the last 24 hours, with a pill when an account must be authorized again or the source is paused. A source page opens with its accounts, then recent events and the flows that handle them, then the receiving settings. A source with no account shows one prompt to add one and lists the events it will bring in. It no longer shows a page of disabled switches.
- On a flow, the description sits under the title. The version history shows the last three versions with their notes, and older ones on request.
- Signals no longer repeats the per-outcome counts above the groups. Each group header shows only its count. By outcome, a row shows who sent the signal instead of repeating the group's outcome. A long reason or step error is cut short until you choose "Show all". The flow, its steps and the original message are now one row of links. When nothing is connected yet, Signals shows how to connect an account and hides the search and filters. When nothing arrived today, it offers the last 7 days.
