- When a message to your own account is handed over to you, its card now reads in words instead of the Hub's ids (`draft_reply`, `to_item`, `done`): **Make it an item** (转成事项), **No reply needed** (不用回复, marks the message handled without sending anything), and a field where you write the reply, sent with **Draft reply** (起草回复). The reply becomes a draft that goes out only after you approve it. Choosing one of these options works again: the card used to answer in a form the Hub rejected.
- The card for a reply that didn't go out now offers every choice the Hub gives it, by name: **Send again** (重新发送), **Don't send it** (不发了) and, when it may already be out, **It went out** (已经发出了). Before, it only offered "retry release", and a send blocked by a missing permission had no button at all.
