- An open turn on a session page (and in an item's work log) can now be read as Steps or on a Timeline. The timeline puts the turn and each thinking stretch, note and tool call on a shared time axis, so you can see where the time went. The slowest finished call is marked, a failed call is red, and a running turn grows live. An interrupted turn says so in its header instead of Done. Each row names the tool and, next to it, Steve's words for what it did, the same as in the steps view. Click a row for its timing, its input and output, or its error. On an item's sessions the timeline header also shows the turn's tokens and estimated cost. These are the Hub's figures for that turn; Steve has no per-call usage, so the rows show time only.
- In the steps view, a single tool call shows the tool's own name, Steve's words for what it did, and how long it took. Opened, it shows what went in (the command, file, query or brief, and for Steve's own tools the arguments as sent) and what came back, each with a copy button. A failed call shows its error. An edit shows its colored diff, and an earlier plan shows its checklist. Calls of the same kind in a row still fold into one line with their subjects as chips.
- While a session runs, the bar above the composer shows a working indicator whose label is the step under way, such as the command it is running. The label rolls over to the next step as the work moves on.
- Chinese durations under a second now read in 毫秒 instead of ms.
