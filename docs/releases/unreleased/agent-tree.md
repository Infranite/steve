- An item that coordinates Bot collaboration or started other items shows its collaboration on the item page, under the "now" card. Each step the coordinator started is one row in dependency order, with its Bot, whether it passed and on which try, and how long its agent worked. A bar on a shared time axis shows when each step ran, so a sequence of steps reads as a staircase and parallel work as overlap. A step tried again stays one row: its earlier attempts are hatched spans on the same bar and are listed with their outcome in the step's detail. Forked items follow the row they came from. The header names the coordinator and counts the steps, the agents, running work, failures (open them to retry) and decisions waiting for you, with the estimated cost of everything under the item. Selecting a row opens its detail and actions beside the page, or as a sheet on narrow screens. This replaces the earlier work list, the item's relation-graph tab and the card grid of the full tree; the relation graph is still in the item menu.
- The collaboration says when part of an item's tokens ran on models without a price, instead of showing a near-zero cost, and shows working time rather than time since the first turn.
