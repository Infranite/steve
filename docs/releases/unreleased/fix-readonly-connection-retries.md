- A read-only channel connection no longer retries and asks about every message. When the channel service forbids sending, for the whole connection or for one chat, the first refused message opens one card that says why and how to allow sending (for example, give the connection's credential the send permission at the channel service). For the next 24 hours Steve writes no automatic replies or acknowledgements there. Messages you, a flow or an agent still send there are kept in the conversation as not sent, without another card. Once the channel service reports that sending is allowed again, or a message there goes through, this ends at once.
- YOLO now resends a message or mail whose send failed at most twice (setting `chan_retry_max` in the YOLO policy). After that, the card stays for you. A send that can never work (a read-only connection, a missing permission) is not resent at all.
