- People can have a photo. On your own page (我) or any contact's page, click the avatar to upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP image up to 10 MB. Steve crops the centre to a square, scales it down to 256 pixels and stores it with the Hub's other files. Click the avatar again to change or remove the photo. Your photo also shows on the account button at the bottom of the sidebar, and contacts' photos show in the contacts list.
- The 我 page and contact pages have a new layout. The top shows the avatar, the name and a single line with the organization and title. A long description is cut to three lines, with 展开 to see the rest. Below that, "Steve 记得的你" lists everything Steve knows about you in one list (preferred name, time zone, language, organization, title, email and any fields you added). You can edit each value right in the list, and each one shows its disclosure level next to it. An empty value shows 补充 to fill it in. The 资料完整性 section is gone. When Steve couldn't fill something in automatically, the reason is shown once under the list, and "no account to look it up with" is no longer repeated on every row. Linked accounts, what you are responsible for, teams, related items, interactions and memories show only when they have something in them. Long memory lists show five at first.
- API: `PUT /api/contacts/:id/avatar` takes the image as the raw request body or as the multipart field `file`, and `DELETE` removes it. Both return the contact, whose `avatar` is now `{"file": "<id>"}`. The image is served by `GET /api/files/:id`. Other image types get 415 and images too large to accept get 413.
