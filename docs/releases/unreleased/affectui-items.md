- The collaboration on the item page is now one list with a row per step: the step's Bot and model, a line saying what it is doing now, on which try it passed, why it failed or what it asks you, a bar for how far it got (green when done, blue while running, red where it stopped, amber while it waits for you), and its working time and cost, earlier tries included. Each row has one action: "去处理" opens the step's item where its decision waits, "重试" retries a failed step through the work graph (with the same revision check as before), and "打开" opens the step's item. What needs you, failed or runs always shows; of the finished steps only the latest three do, the rest behind "显示其余 N 步". Selecting a row still opens its detail beside the page, with its earlier tries. The header counts what needs you, failed, runs and is done (the first two open their lists, with retry), and keeps the coordinator, the number of steps, the elapsed time against the agents' working time, the estimated cost and the earlier-round tag. The lanes view is gone.
- "查看全图" in that header opens the whole collaboration in a large dialog (a sheet on phones) as one dependency graph of its steps. Each step is a small card with its Bot, its title, how it ended (完成, 运行中, 等你, 失败, 已停止, 已暂停 or 未开始), "第 N 次通过" or "重试过 N 次" when it was tried before (earlier tries are a stack behind the card, not cards of their own), and its working time and cost. An arrow points from a step to the one that waits for it, only where no other path already says so; it is dashed while the earlier step is not done yet. Steps that could run at the same time share a column (a row on phones), so a chain reads as one line, and a long chain wraps into a second band instead of shrinking. The header names the coordinator with the number of steps, the elapsed time against the agents' working time, the estimated cost, the retries and the failed steps. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom; the view fits itself when it opens. Selecting a step opens its detail beside the graph (below it on phones): what this try and all tries took, its goal and report (Markdown, long text folded), its outputs (an output its item keeps opens in the output viewer), its checks, why it failed, its earlier tries, the steps it waited for and the ones that wait for it, buttons to open its item and session and to retry or rework it, and, folded until asked for, its tool calls turn by turn on a timeline, loaded only then.
- The items page has a fourth view, "协作": every open item that coordinates steps is a card with its state, cost and working time; open one and its steps hang beside it with their Bot, state, cost and time, a waiting step with its question and a failed one with its reason. Selecting a step opens its item.
- These are built on the Parallel Attempts and Agent Fleet components from affectUI Pro (the graph uses the same graph library as the relation graph), which are licensed separately from Steve; each source file says so on its first line.
- On a session page, the turns listed in the "工作中的 agent" card end in an ellipsis inside the card instead of running past its edge.
