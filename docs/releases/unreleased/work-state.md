- A turn you stop yourself is now recorded with its session. After a Hub restart you still get a single "It stopped: you interrupted this turn" card, and neither Steve nor YOLO continues the work for you. If you stop a coordinator after it was told that a work unit failed, the card also gives the failure's reason, and no continue is spent. A late end of an earlier turn no longer takes that away. Steve continues as usual once your next message starts another turn. Item diagnostics now also show how the session's last turn ended and whether you stopped it.
- The end of a turn is handled once per attempt, also after a restart: a turn end delivered again after a Hub restart neither takes your Stop away nor lets Steve or YOLO continue. When you stop a newer turn, a late end of the previous one does not use up your Stop. A Stop whose delivery could not be confirmed (the Node did not answer, say) still counts as yours until you send a new message.
- Pausing a work graph marks the exact turns it stopped. If you resume at once, only those turns' ends count as the pause, also across a Hub restart: the unit goes on in its session instead of showing "work unit interrupted". A later turn you stop yourself still fails its unit.
- Stop cards the work has moved past expire on every check, also right after a restart: a coordinator's stop card while its units run, queue, wait for you or are paused, and the leftover questions and stop cards of a unit that failed or was cancelled. Cards about an effect nobody knows, a release, an approval or an acceptance stay. An item's own stop card no longer keeps a voided delivery from reaching its agent.
- A work unit out of attempts no longer leaves the review and integration units after it "queued" forever: the coordinator is told that the unit is out of attempts and what it blocks. A cancelled unit's own item now closes as cancelled once its turn ends (or at the next check after a restart) instead of staying active. A retried unit's old attempt that reported a failure keeps "failed" and its reason in the history, and its late end closes the old child as failed without touching the new attempt.
- Steve and YOLO recognise more reliably when an agent waits for something only you can do. The option a model picks for a question is checked too (an option claiming you already confirmed on your phone is never submitted for you); the agent's whole final reply is read, so "scan the code and tell me when done" at the start of a long reply is not missed; plain requests such as "Please log in to GitHub and tell me when done", "enter the verification code to continue" and "use your passkey" are left to you. Design questions and reports about codes, OTP lengths, passkeys or QR components are answered as usual. A request to scan that the agent then worked around with a tool, saying nothing after it, no longer counts as waiting for you.
- Finished work units no longer show "Stop not yet confirmed". The note shows only on a cancelled or failed unit whose execution has not been confirmed over. A unit that ended normally no longer blocks replanning, retrying or completing the item while its Node is offline.
