- Steve's questions and decisions have a new card, in the conversation, in Pending and on an item's page. The question comes first, then numbered choices: click one or press its number key. The last row takes your own words, and it does what that card allows: answer in your own words, say what to rework, reject, with or without a reason, or add a note before continuing. Every answer that was there before is still offered, including "Accept and release", "Rewrite, then show me", "Remind me later", withdrawing the card and dropping the item.
- A tool permission is a card of its own. It shows the command and its arguments, with Deny and Allow once. Where the agent offers it, a checkbox allows that kind of call from now on. ⌘↵ allows while the card has focus; denying always takes the Deny button.
- The line above the chat composer that counted the decisions waiting for you is now the same card, folded to one line. Show opens it and you can answer there, paging through the decisions; Hide folds it again. A decision the conversation already shows in place is not repeated there. Decisions that need their full view, such as mail drafts, contacts and flows, offer a row that opens them.
- "While you were away" is a compact card. It says when you left and counts what waits on you, what failed and what got done since then, with one action per row: Decide brings up the decisions above the composer, Look and Open go to the item. Got it folds it into a single line. Items that are still running are no longer listed there; the side panel shows them.
- The actions under Steve's replies (Copy, Quote reply, Make an item, Answer here instead) are a quieter bar with tooltips, and Copy confirms with a check. While Steve works on a reply, its current phase shows next to a small spinner and changes in place.
