- The Stats page is reorganised around the questions it answers. After the range, project and Bot filters comes one line with what is running, waiting on you or failed right now, and the YOLO state. Click a count to see its items. Then come the key numbers for the range: items done, estimated cost, agent working time, time waiting on you, first-pass rate and average cost per done item. Each is compared with the previous range of the same length, and a number with no data is left out.
- Daily output shows items done each day as bars, with a line for the estimated cost, the agent turns or the agent time (a turn counts on the day it ended). Click a day for the items done and created that day.
- "Where the money and time went" splits the cost, or the agent time, by item, Bot, model, harness or purpose: at most five named parts, the rest folded into one. It also lists the most expensive items. When nothing in the range has a price, it splits the tokens and says so.
- Two sortable tables compare Bots and harness · model pairs. The Bot table has items done, first-pass rate, failures, rework, turn errors, time and cost per item, cache hit rate and tokens per item. Click a Bot to see the page for that Bot only. The harness · model table has turns, success rate, retries, average turn time, cost per turn, cost, cache hit rate and tokens. The lead-time trend, the quality and agent panels and the separate usage table are gone. Their key figures are in the key numbers and these tables.
- At the bottom, folded until opened, the event explorer shows a histogram of every agent turn, decision card and item milestone in the range, with failures marked. Drag to choose a time window, then slide it, resize it or zoom in. The list below shows only the events in the window. The arrow keys, Shift-arrow, Enter and Esc work too.
- An item's statistics page shows where its cost went. Every turn is a step, and the items it started (such as the steps of a collaboration) are folded groups, so the bars add up to the total. A switch measures tokens instead, and the most expensive step is named. Opening a step shows its timing, stop reason, tools, token split and cost. Above it, the item's timeline has three lanes (turns, decisions, milestones), with a dashed line at the current time while the item is open.
- Tokens on models without a price are shown, not hidden. The items table and an item's figures read, for example, "— · 7.4M unpriced", with the priced part as a lower bound, instead of "<$0.01".
- Devices: the Hub's card shows load, memory, the fullest disk and running sessions, orange from 75% and red from 90%. A device page opens with a status card that gives each metric a 24-hour sparkline. The YOLO record shows each time YOLO was on as a card with its figures, and a permitted command once.
- API: `GET /api/stats/series` has new metrics: `active` (distinct items worked on), `turn_ms` (agent turn time, grouped like `turns`) and `wait_ms` (time a person was waited on, cards answered by YOLO excluded, grouped by `none`, `bot` or `project`). `GET /api/stats/events` feeds the event explorer, and `GET /api/stats/tree/{id}` returns an item and the items started from it, each with its own usage and agent time.
