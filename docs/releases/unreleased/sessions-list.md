- The 会话 list reads as a log. Running conversations and those waiting on you sit in their own group at the top, and the rest are grouped by day. Each conversation takes one line with its item number, title, model, context used and time in aligned columns. A harness, device, source, bot or model that every conversation shares is no longer repeated on each row, and `#1` is dropped. The filters show their counts and hide when they would match nothing, the harness filter appears only when there is more than one harness, and 运行中 becomes 进行中 so conversations waiting on you are included. Long histories show 60 at a time.
- Above the 会话 list, an activity heatmap shows how busy each day was, darker for a busier day, over 7 days, 1 month, 3 months or 1 year (the choice is remembered). Each span is laid out to fill the width: 7 days is a row per day and a square per hour, 1 or 3 months a row per month and a square per day, and a year GitHub's calendar of week columns. The grids are @nivo/heatmap and react-activity-calendar; pointing at a square gives its figure. 任务 counts the distinct items an agent worked on that day, with a conversation that has no item counted on its own; Token shows the tokens used. The figures come from the new `active` metric of `GET /api/stats/series` and the existing `tokens` metric; a Hub without the stats module shows no heatmap.
