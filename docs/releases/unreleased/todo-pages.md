- New **待办** page (`#/todos`): your own todos grouped into 逾期, 今天, 本周, 以后 and 没定日期, with the todos you handed to a Bot and the ones you snoozed. Type a line such as “周五前 交 Q4 复盘 18:00 #Q4复盘” and a day, a time, a goal and a priority (`!1`–`!3`) are read out of it by fixed rules, shown as chips you can remove. Tick a todo off, snooze it to this evening, tomorrow or next Monday, drop it, or hand it to a Bot, which creates an item; every change can be undone from its notice.
- Goals: the **目标** view and each goal's page show its progress (done out of everything not dropped, or “还没拆” before anything is under it), the todos and linked items under it, and let you add a step or link an existing item. A todo's page edits its deadline, repeat, goal, priority and star.
- New **日历** page: until calendar sync is available it says which calendars are planned, links to 连接, and lists your todos due in the next 7 days.
- The sidebar's main group is now 对话 · 信息流 · 待处理 · 日历 · 待办 · 事项 · 项目, with a gray count beside 待办 for what is due today or overdue; 会话 and 统计 moved to the 自动化 group. On a phone the tab bar is 对话 · 待处理 · 日历 · 待办 · 事项, and 我的 opens from your avatar at the top left of each page, with 信息流 as its first row. ⌘K has 新待办 and the new pages.
