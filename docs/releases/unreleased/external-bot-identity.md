External channel and connector services can now connect explicitly as a bot. A plugin's `identity: "bot"` selects the bot provider, and its hello and account declarations must agree. Bot handles stay on a bot contact rather than “me”, bot sends use the normal bot outbox with stable delivery receipts, and bot connections cannot act as you. Existing services that omit identity keep the self behavior, including own-message suppression and approved replies. The public deployment guide documents the identity and write contracts. This is a generic prerequisite only: no built-in platform adapter has been moved, and notification defaults and existing platform adoption workflows are unchanged.
