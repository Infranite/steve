- The web client has a new look. It uses Geist with the system CJK face, one accent colour on cool neutrals, a fixed type and radius scale, soft elevation instead of nested frames, and one page frame, so titles line up on every page. Motion follows one scale: 120 ms for colour and press, 180 ms for menus, toggles and page changes, 260 ms for dialogs, sheets and anything that opens or folds.
- Buttons, view switchers, filter chips, checkboxes, switches, text fields, selects, search boxes, badges, spinners, skeletons, cards and empty states look and behave the same on every page, in light and dark mode. Selects take type-ahead and never get cut off. Date pickers are small calendars in your language. Number fields have − and + buttons and stay within their limits.
- Dialogs, confirmations and side sheets share one look. On a phone or in a narrow window they slide up from the bottom across the full width. Menus have one soft highlight that follows the pointer and the arrow keys. Tooltips show after a short rest of the pointer, or at once under keyboard focus. Toasts stack, can be swiped away and appear just above the composer, so they no longer cover Send or Stop; F8 moves the focus to the newest.
- The sidebar is a flat app shell. At the top are your workspace with its menu and a button that folds the sidebar to a rail of icons (⌘B still works). Below come the Hub, the YOLO line, a running-status pill ("1 in progress · 2 waiting on you", which opens the open items with their current step and lets you pause, resume or open them) and a search field. At the bottom is your account: name, role in this workspace, and a menu with 我, Devices, Accounts, Settings and Sign out. In a short window the lower groups scroll between the header and the footer.
- Pages under another page (an item, a device, a project, a contact) show a breadcrumb in their top bar. On a phone the tab bar takes the sidebar's colour.
- New names: 接入 is now 连接 (Connections), 渠道 is now 群聊与私聊 (Chats), and the generic connector for chat apps other than Feishu, formerly 外部渠道 (External channel), is now 其他聊天工具 (Other chat app). **Capabilities** (能力) is a new entry next to Bots.
- Search (⌘K) is a command menu. Before you type, it lists actions and pages, then items, sessions, projects and Bots. Once you type, it finds items and sessions by title or number, sessions whose transcript mentions the text, messages of the main conversation (selecting one jumps to it), and contacts and teams. Tab jumps between groups.
- The first-run tour in the main conversation is one card in four steps: a hello, the runtimes found on this machine, the projects of the current space, and the first meeting (for the instance's owner) or a first task (for anyone else). You can go back to a finished step. Settings · About · Onboarding starts it again.
- Many pages read better on a phone: tabs move below the title, contact details and option values wrap instead of running past the edge, and lists reach the right edge of the page. Rows that hold their own buttons no longer put a button inside a button.
- The component showroom at `#/design/components` shows every component in its states, in both themes. Some components come from affectUI Pro, which is licensed separately from Steve. Each such source file says so on its first line.
