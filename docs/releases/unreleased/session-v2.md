- A conversation's page is redesigned. The transcript and the composer form one centred column between the sidebar and the side panel at every width. The side panel (a sheet on narrower screens, from the bottom on a phone) is one quiet column without cards: sections set apart by space and a small label, and rows of a short label beside its value. It shows only what has been reported:
  - The head: the harness and model, the conversation's state, its number and the item it belongs to.
  - Figures: how full the context window is, as a thin bar with the share and the tokens (ⓘ opens the harness's own context-window view and a note on what the harness reports); the estimated cost, with the harness's own figure when that differs; how long it worked, in how many turns and their average; how old it is, when it started and when it was last active (for an imported transcript, when it was imported and its number of turns); and its tokens with the share of cache reads, input and output ("Details" opens the split by kind with the estimate and the cache hit rate).
  - Outputs: the outputs it delivered to its item (open one to preview it), the first three and the rest on request, and the files it changed.
  - Relations: its item and the item's state, what is upstream (a collaboration's lead item, the item it was forked from, prerequisites), what is downstream as a count that opens into the list (collaboration units, items forked from it, items waiting on it) and the item's other conversations. The full graph is one click away.
  - Knowledge used: each document once, with its source, its level and how many of its snippets came along; the search that found it shows on hover.
  - Runtime, folded: Bot, harness, model, reasoning effort, device, source and working directory, and how to resume the conversation in its harness.
- Harnesses are shown with their marks: Claude Code with the Claude mark, Codex with the OpenAI mark, and any other harness with a terminal mark. The marks appear in the conversation's header, the composer's label, the read-only runtime line, the conversation list and its harness filter and the conversation rows on an item's page. Model names in the context-window view, the runtime details and the list's model column show their maker's mark.
- The conversation header now also shows how much of the context is in use (amber from 80%). In the transcript, each finished turn of an item's conversation shows its tokens and estimated cost.
