对话 has a channel switcher: Steve, then each channel account you can see (the workspace's bots, and your own account for you alone), with how many of its conversations have new messages. Picking an account opens its groups and private chats, most recent first, each with the time of its last message and how many new messages came in since you last opened it there (counted back at most 7 days). The list can also be filtered by account.

A channel conversation now shows the chat's whole record, not only the messages Steve was handed: every message you may see, received and sent, in order, with earlier messages a page at a time. Under each received message it says what Steve did with it: answered, waiting for you (with a button to the card), handed off to an item (with a link to it), working, skipped or only recorded (with the reason it was recorded with), or failed with the reason, for example a trigger word whose answer could not start. A message Steve sent that did not go out says why, with a button to retry or discard it. A space where Steve never answered is listed too. Opening a conversation marks it read for you only.
