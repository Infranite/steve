A trigger word that needs the machine is now handed to a Bot instead of failing silently. When you ask with `/ai` for something only the machine can tell, such as how much disk is left, the quick answer has no tools. Its model reaches for one and the call refuses it. Steve now makes the request an item, once, and a Bot does the work on a device. The reply in the thread says so, and the item reports its progress there. This applies only to trigger words you send yourself on your own account. When someone else uses your trigger word for such a request, the chat gets the short failure note described below. In your conversation in Steve and in chats a Bot answers, such a question still gets an answer that says why it can't be answered directly; "做成事项" turns it into an item as before. A chat where Steve may not start work, such as one with outside members, now says in its conversation why there is no answer and what to do instead. For a trigger word's answer, the next model in the list is no longer tried once a model reached for a tool. Every other model call, such as routing, mail triage and summaries, still tries the next model.

When a trigger word gets no answer, you can see why. The failure is recorded in that chat's conversation in Steve with the actual error, and a short note goes to the chat thread from your account ("这次没能处理…"). The note doesn't contain the details. When someone else used your trigger word, the note speaks as you ("抱歉，这次没能自动答上来，我看到后再回复你。"). Your account may be unable to send the note or the answer, for example because a permission is missing. Steve then tells you once, in the notifications and in your conversation, which account cannot send and that you can allow sending for it under Connections. It doesn't tell you again for every message. After your account sends again, you are told about the next failure. When the channel service refuses sending for good (a read-only connection), the card of the first refused message is that one notice, and no second one is added. Before, a trigger word that couldn't be routed, or that came from someone else on an account without permission to send as you, ended with no trace. It is now recorded in that chat's conversation too.
