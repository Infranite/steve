A trigger word that needs the machine is now handed to a Bot instead of failing silently. When you ask with `/ai` for something only the machine can tell, such as how much disk is left, the quick answer has no tools. Its model reaches for one and the call refuses it. Steve now makes the request an item, once, and a Bot does the work on a device. The reply in the thread says so, and the item reports its progress there. A chat where Steve may not start work, such as one with outside members, now says in its conversation why there is no answer and what to do instead. The next model in the list is no longer tried for a request that needs tools.

When a trigger word gets no answer, you can see why. The failure is recorded in that chat's conversation in Steve, and a short note goes to the chat thread from your account ("这次没能处理…"). The note doesn't contain the details. Your account may be unable to send, for example when the channel service only allows reading. Steve then tells you once, in the notifications and in your conversation, which account cannot send and that you can allow sending for it under Connections. It doesn't tell you again for every message. After your account sends again, you are told about the next failure. Before, a trigger word that couldn't be routed, or that came from someone else on an account without permission to send as you, ended with no trace. It is now recorded in that chat's conversation too.
