- Every memory on the Knowledge page now says what it is about and where it came from, and both are links: the person or project opens its subject view, and the source opens the day of the main conversation, the item, the agent session, the mail thread or the event it was learned from. Hovering over the source shows who noted it down and the words it was based on. The line also says how many times Steve has used the memory. Memories waiting in To confirm link to their source the same way.
- A new Subjects tab lists the people, projects and Bots Steve knows something about, with how many memories, documents and unconfirmed memories each has. Opening one shows what Steve knows about it and where it learned it: which sources the memories came from, the long-term memories and knowledge entries, the documents about it, and the sessions, answers and searches that used them lately. A project also gathers its item records and the memories its items' deliveries left.
- The Sources tab shows each document source's state (OK, syncing, error or paused) next to its counts and last sync, and built-in sources say they follow their originals as they change. A new section, Where memories come from, lists who writes the memories (you, the main conversation, item deliveries, agent sessions, a Bot, mail, plugins) with how many each wrote and how many still wait for you. It also says whether search finds things by meaning with OpenViking or uses the built-in keyword index.
- A new Tidy-up tab runs a knowledge steward on a schedule: it merges memories that say the same thing, settles contradictions (the newest explicit correction wins), marks outdated ones stale, and fills in who a memory is about or which item it came from. Turn it on and pick how often it runs (daily, weekly or monthly) on that tab, or tidy up now. The steward is an ordinary Bot created from the new Knowledge steward Bot template, on an ordinary schedule, so you can change its rules on its Bot page and its time under Automation › Schedules.
- Each tidy-up run is listed with every change it made and its reason. A merge waits for you in To confirm and replaces the memories it names once you confirm it. A conflict the steward couldn't settle asks you which memory to keep. Every other change has Undo, and an answered conflict can be reopened.
- Agents have two new tools for this, available to every harness: `steve_memory_list` lists the memories with their sources and use counts, and `steve_memory_tidy` makes the changes above. Changes any agent makes with them show up in the Tidy-up tab under the item they came from.
