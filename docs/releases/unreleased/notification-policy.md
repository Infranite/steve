Added the first backend-only personal notification slice for real durable work-completion events. Members explicitly opt in to their own now/digest/log policy and may select a bot DM plus time-zone-aware quiet hours. Now notifications recover through the existing output-intent/channel sender and delivery ledger; digest/log only retain inbox entries and never send. Quiet hours postpone pending sends across midnight and daylight-saving transitions. Live consent, membership, the exact self-linked recipient and the pinned route are rechecked before sending or retrying; an uncertain send is not blindly repeated.

Authenticated personal policy and inbox APIs isolate each member's read state. Event/member entries commit atomically with durable-consumer receipts, and their delivery link commits with the existing outbound message. Body retention and channel cleanup also cover notification copies without losing replay tombstones. This does not add other event types, scheduled digest generation/subscriptions, an activity-feed UI, learned notification policy, escalation, upgrade notifications or email delivery.
