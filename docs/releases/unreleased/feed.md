Steve now has an independent Feed for scheduled reports and opted-in personal completion notifications, separate from chat and decisions in the Inbox. New reports go to Feed instead of posting their full text to chat; historical conversations stay unchanged. Shared reports have per-person read state, and personal notifications stay private to their recipient.

Feed supports unread and source filters, full Markdown, loading beyond the first 100 entries, selected read operations and snapshot-bounded “mark all read” that leaves concurrent new arrivals unread. Notification settings continue to control consent, external delivery and quiet hours; reading neither sends messages nor resolves decisions. Existing notification content retention applies without keeping a second body copy in Feed.

The sidebar and phone navigation no longer label Steve's main features as only “Work”. This does not add RSS collection, personal report subscriptions or scheduled digest generation.
