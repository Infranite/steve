- A Bot's page now opens on an Overview that is useful even for a Bot with nothing in progress. It shows who the Bot is (role, harness and model with their marks, way of speaking, proactivity), its last 30 days from the statistics (turns, items done, first-pass rate, average time and cost per item, cache hit rate), its items with running ones first and then the latest finished, and a summary of its capabilities, permissions, acceptance, system prompt and memories. Each line opens the tab where you change it. A Bot that has not done any work yet shows a short "Start configuring" guide instead: write its role, choose its harness and model, see what it can do.
- The System prompt tab has a "Tell Steve how to change it…" field. Steve drafts a new version of the working rules (when the prompt is generated) or of your own prompt, and shows it as a before/after diff. Nothing is saved until you press Apply. An empty prompt of your own offers "Let Steve draft it". For integrations: `POST /api/config/draft` takes an optional `field` (`rules` or `prompt`) and `base` (the text to start from) on a `bot` target, and the draft's `patch` carries the whole new text.
- Harness and model choices show the harness's and the model maker's marks, in the field and in the list: in a Bot's settings, in the New Bot dialog and in Settings → Models.
- The model menu opens under its field at a normal width again, with its list scrolling inside, instead of stretching across the whole window. Any popover whose trigger sits in a form field had the same problem and is fixed too.
- Codex Bots can pick a model before the Node has reported Codex's own list: Codex now declares a built-in list (gpt-6.1-sol, gpt-6-sol, gpt-6-luna, gpt-5.6-luna), as Claude Code does. What the adapter reports still replaces it once the Node has probed it.
