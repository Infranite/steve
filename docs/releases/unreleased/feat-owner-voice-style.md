- Drafts on your own chat account are written as you, not as the Bot behind the account. The Bot now only picks the model there; its name, duty, manner and working rules are no longer in the prompt, and neither are the item numbers of the person who wrote.
- The composer is shown a few messages you typed yourself on that account: from the same chat first, then from chats of the same kind, most recent first. Links, attachments, placeholders, redactions and anything shaped like a key are left out. A message from another chat is used only when it is short and has no digits, no @ and no name of anyone in your chats, so nothing said in one chat reaches another. It also gets your habits in this kind of situation (a private chat, a group where someone @-ed or answered you, a group): how long your messages usually are, how often they end in a full stop or ask something, how you open them and which emoji you use. These are computed from your recent messages each time a reply is drafted, so they follow how you talk.
- Drafts you rejected are shown as "not like this", and drafts you edited before sending are shown, as you sent them, as "like this". A draft approved unchanged or by YOLO is not used.
- The rules of writing as you: one or two short lines, no "收到 / 好的" openers, no promises on your behalf (those come to you instead), at most one question and only when needed, no options to pick from, no mention of Steve, items or queues, nothing that contradicts what you already said, and when there is nothing solid to say, something short or nothing.
- After a draft is written as you, it is checked: a list of options, more than one question, a draft more than twice as long as your usual long messages in that situation, or an opener you never use. A draft that fails goes back once with what was wrong; if the second one still fails, nothing is drafted and the reason is kept. Replies on bot accounts are not checked this way.
