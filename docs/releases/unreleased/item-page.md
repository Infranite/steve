The six places that used to state an item's status, and sometimes disagreed, now read the same state. The conversation folds runs of process notes to one line and shows them without Markdown marks. A long goal folds to its first three lines. The activity is grouped under day headings and shows the clock time on each line; two or more process notes in a row fold to one line. Progress and properties move to a side column, with rarely used properties folded. The "now" card says in plain words what an earlier round of collaboration means and lists why the item cannot finish yet.
- The items list, the inbox, contact and team pages, the session page and the composer show the same item state as the item page, so an item whose move is yours is grouped under 等你 everywhere. A work graph from an earlier cycle no longer changes the item's state: the item goes on in its current cycle.
- Chat item cards, the chat rail, the stats overview and the Bot pages count and label items by the same state as the item page. Repeated notices (the same disk warning again) fold into one line with the others under it; research thoughts in the rail are named by their answer; the sessions list says once what all sessions share.
