- The settings pages use one set of controls: choices are a field-style picker with a list that is never cut off, switches slide with a stretching thumb, and text fields share one look across Workspace, Access and clients, Vault and Stats. In dark mode the fields keep a visible fill and edge, and on a phone a picker sits under its setting at full width.
- Workspace settings save on their own: the name and item prefix sit as rows with their field on the right, the description has a field of its own below, and each saves when you leave it, so there is no save bar any more. Saving one field never replaces what you are typing in another, Escape puts a field back, and a save that fails keeps your text and marks it as not saved.
- Notifications show each message as a switch: turn the daily summary or quiet hours on and its time unfolds under it; turning one off stores "don't send" or "off" as before, and turning it back on brings back the time you had.
- Clearing all bot memories moved to a danger zone at the bottom of Data and privacy, and confirming it opens in place under the row instead of a dialog. The focus starts on Cancel, so pressing Enter twice clears nothing; Escape or Cancel folds it back.
