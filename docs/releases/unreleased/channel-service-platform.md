A channel service (其他聊天工具, or a channel plugin) can now say which platform it reaches. Its connection is then listed under that platform on the 接入 page instead of under 其他聊天工具. A plugin that proxies your own Feishu account appears under 飞书 / Lark as its own 代你处理, with no bot needed and nothing said to be missing. It is independent of any Feishu bot you connect. The connection is regrouped the next time it connects; nothing has to be connected again.
