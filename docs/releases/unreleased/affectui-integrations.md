- The Integrations page is now a directory of the services Steve can connect. Each service is a card that says whether it is connected, what it can do today, and flags any connection that needs attention, such as missing permissions or an expired authorization; what is planned is on the service's page. You can search the services, filter them to connected or not connected, and browse them by capability (channels, sending as you, mail, code repositories, directory). Selecting a card opens the service's page, where connecting still follows that service's own steps.
- A connection on an account page is now one settings card. Its switch pauses or resumes receiving. Its sections show any problem with a re-authorize button, the capabilities Steve may use and a link to the channel identity. A mailbox's settings are part of the same card. Its send delay, archiving and sync range save as soon as you change them, like every other switch on the card. Its signature and duty are saved from a save bar right under them, with a button to discard the edits; while they are unsaved the connection can't be paused, and the browser asks before you close or reload the page. Checking the connection, replacing its credentials and disconnecting stay at the bottom of the card, even while it is paused.
- Each installation on a service's page (a Feishu app, a GitHub token, a mailbox) uses the same card design.
