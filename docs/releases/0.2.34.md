# Steve v0.2.34

Steve now proposes far fewer things to remember, and each one says what it would become and why. A one-off request, a restated goal or a preference guessed from a single remark stays with its item or conversation and no longer becomes a confirm card. (ST-41)

- Extraction sorts the text into four kinds: 事项记录, 会话记录, 长期记忆 and 知识条目. Only the last two are proposed, at most 3 per source. Proposing nothing is the normal outcome.
- The server checks every candidate:
  - its quote must be in the text;
  - a preference or rule needs a lasting signal ("以后", "后续", "每次", "一律", "之前说过" …) or two separate quotes;
  - candidates that restate the item's goal are dropped;
  - candidates close to a confirmed or still pending entry are dropped.
- Items and the main thread no longer yield events. Reworks sent back automatically by YOLO are no longer read as your words.
- The confirm card asks "存为长期记忆吗？" or "存为知识条目吗？" and shows the type, why it is worth keeping and where it applies. Its buttons are 不保存 / 保存. Cards opened earlier still work.
- 知识 has separate 长期记忆 and 知识条目 tabs, and 待确认 shows each candidate's type and reason. Citations read 事项记录 / 会话记录 / 长期记忆 / 知识条目, and existing index labels are renamed once.
- The tool description of `steve_remember` now says the same: only lasting rules, preferences and verified facts.
