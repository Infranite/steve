# Steve v0.2.9

This release makes Steve feel faster: less to download, fewer full refreshes, steadier chat scrolling, and Feishu messages that arrive sooner and completely.

- The web app loads in pieces. The first screen needs about 330 kB of compressed script instead of 565 kB, and the Hub now gzips scripts, styles and JSON answers (the main bundle drops from 1.84 MB to about 0.56 MB on the wire). Pages you don't open are fetched when you open them; after a release, a page whose old pieces are gone reloads itself once.
- Live updates no longer redraw the whole app while an agent streams: only the session that changed is updated. Pages that refresh on events still refresh at least once a second under a steady stream, and conversation pages ignore events from other conversations.
- After a dropped connection the app resynchronises everything it shows (state, open items, loaded transcripts and module pages) instead of only part of it.
- Long sessions show their first page of events right away instead of waiting for the whole history, and opening an item loads the active session first.
- Chats only follow new messages while you are at the bottom; scrolled up, a "new messages" button appears instead of pulling you down. Sending a message or answering a card shows that it is being submitted and cannot be sent twice.
- Feishu: groups a bot sits in were skipped by your own polling even when the bot could only hear @-mentions, so other messages in those groups never arrived. They are read again unless the bot is healthy and has the "receive all group messages" permission. Your chats are polled every 20 seconds (10 seconds right after a new private message, 2 minutes in quiet hours) instead of every minute.
- The Hub logs API calls slower than 300 ms with their route, so slowness can be traced.

No database migration (schema 26). Hub and Web must come from the same release. No new provider permissions are requested.
